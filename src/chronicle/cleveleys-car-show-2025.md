## Introduction

<a href="https://classiccarclubs.uk/club-events/cleveleys-classic-vehicle-show-2025/">The Cleveleys Car Show 2025</a> is set to be a marquee automotive event, scheduled for 8th June 2025, from 9 AM to 4:30 PM. This annual gathering is renowned for transforming the town centre and promenade of Cleveleys, Lancashire, into a vibrant celebration of automotive culture. Sponsored by Crystal Clear Details, the event promises to be a spectacular showcase of classic and modern vehicles, attracting enthusiasts from across the region and beyond.

## Event Details

The Cleveleys Car Show will take place along Victoria Road West and the picturesque seaside promenade, offering a unique backdrop for the display of hundreds of vehicles. This free event is designed to be a family-friendly affair, providing an exciting day out for attendees of all ages. The show will feature a diverse array of cars, from classic models to the latest high-performance vehicles, ensuring there is something to captivate every automotive enthusiast.

One of the most notable aspects of the event is its location along the scenic Sea Front Promenade, providing a picturesque backdrop that enhances the visual appeal of the cars on display. This unique setting allows visitors to enjoy both the beauty of the automobiles and the natural surroundings.

## History and Origins

The Cleveleys Car Show has established itself as a significant attraction for car enthusiasts and families alike. The event has been consistently held in early June, typically on the second Sunday of the month, capitalising on the warmer weather and increased foot traffic during the summer season.

Driven by the passion of its organisers, led by [Russell Larcombe of UK Wheels Events](https://ukwheelsevents.ning.com/events), for vehicles of various types, the show has rapidly grown in popularity since its inception. Although specific details about its first year are not extensively documented, the event has evolved into a community gathering where local businesses and sponsors, particularly Crystal Clear Details, play an integral role in its execution.

The involvement of local sponsors highlights the community-oriented nature of the show, fostering a sense of local pride and economic activity that has become a hallmark of the event.

## Logistics and Planning

### Venue and Accessibility

The event will be held at the town centre and promenade in Cleveleys, Lancashire. The location is easily accessible via public transport and provides ample parking for visitors arriving by car. The strategic positioning along the coast not only enhances the visual appeal of the event but also supports local businesses by drawing visitors to nearby shops, cafés, and restaurants.

### Registration and Costs

Vehicle registration for exhibitors is conducted in advance, with a fee of £12 per vehicle. For those preferring to register on the day, tickets will be available at the gate for £15 per vehicle. This structured registration process ensures a seamless experience for both exhibitors and attendees.

## Potential Exhibitors and Attractions

The show is expected to feature a wide range of exhibitors, showcasing vehicles from different eras and categories. Notable marques such as McLaren, Ferrari, and Porsche are anticipated to participate, highlighting the evolution of automotive technology, particularly in the realm of hybrid and high-performance cars.

The show is characterised by its diverse range of vehicles, which includes classic cars, vintage models, and contemporary designs. This diversity attracts a wide audience, from dedicated car enthusiasts to families looking for a fun day out. In addition to individual exhibitors, car clubs are encouraged to register and display their collections, fostering a sense of community among enthusiasts.

## Economic and Community Impact

The Cleveleys Car Show plays a significant role in boosting the local economy by attracting visitors to the area. The influx of attendees provides a substantial economic boost to local businesses, including hospitality, retail, and service sectors. Furthermore, the event fosters community spirit by bringing together residents and visitors in a shared celebration of automotive culture.

The event's inclusive nature is emphasised by its free admission, ensuring accessibility to all members of the community. The show's emphasis on inclusivity and accessibility ensures that it remains a beloved annual tradition for the Cleveleys community.

## Event Significance

The significance of the Cleveleys Car Show extends beyond its display of automobiles. It serves as a community event that brings together residents and visitors, enhancing the local economy and providing a platform for small businesses to showcase their offerings. The event transforms the town centre and promenade into a vibrant exhibition space, contributing to the cultural heritage of Cleveleys.

The show offers a glimpse into the evolution of automotive technology and design, serving as a celebration of automotive history with vehicles ranging from classic cars to modern models, providing a comprehensive overview of the automotive industry.

## Roundup

The Cleveleys Car Show 2025 is poised to be a highlight of the automotive calendar, offering a dynamic blend of classic and contemporary vehicles set against the stunning backdrop of the Cleveleys coastline. Building upon years of tradition whilst maintaining its community-focused spirit, the show continues to evolve, ensuring its place as a cherished local tradition.
