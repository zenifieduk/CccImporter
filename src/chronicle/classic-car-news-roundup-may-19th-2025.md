## Classic Car News UK Roundup – May 19th 2025

The classic car market in the UK continues to thrive, with exciting auctions, busy show calendars, and new challenges for owners and enthusiasts.
  
### Safety in the Spotlight

The classic car community is in mourning following the tragic incident at Bicester Motion, which has underscored the importance of safety and risk management at motoring venues. As enthusiasts and organisers reflect on the event, there is renewed focus on ensuring robust safety measures to protect both vehicles and people at shows, auctions, and club gatherings across the country.

### Market Trends and Challenges

**Legislative Impacts**  
The upcoming ban on petrol and diesel cars, due in 2030, is a pressing issue. Owners are weighing their options, with many considering electric conversions to secure the future of their vehicles. As classic cars become increasingly cherished, rising running costs may shift them further into luxury territory.
