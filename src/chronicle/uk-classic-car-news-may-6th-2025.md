## Introduction

The classic car scene in the United Kingdom is thriving, with a wealth of events, auctions, and commemorations taking place across the country. Today, on May 6, 2025, we delve into the latest happenings in the classic car world, highlighting key events, trends, and notable sales that are captivating enthusiasts and collectors alike.

## Classic Car Gatherings and Events

One of the most anticipated events in the classic car calendar is the annual show in Stroud, where enthusiasts gather to celebrate their shared passion. This year, the event attracted a significant number of classic car lovers who showcased a variety of vintage and timeless vehicles. The event not only provided a platform for enthusiasts to display their prized possessions but also offered a unique opportunity for families and car aficionados to experience the elegance and charm of historical vehicles ([Airey](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/pictures-classic-car-lovers-gather-110000832.html)).

In addition to the Stroud show, the UK is hosting a series of classic car events throughout 2025, offering enthusiasts a chance to embark on a nostalgic journey. These events are spread across regions such as Avon, Cambridgeshire, Cornwall, Buckinghamshire, and Devon, providing ample opportunities for local communities to engage with the classic car culture ([Car Events LTD](https://www.carevents.com/uk/classic-car-events/)).

## Trends in Classic Car Auctions

Recent auction sales have highlighted a significant trend in the classic car market: cars that were tuned in-period by aftermarket companies are seeing a substantial increase in value compared to their unmodified counterparts. This trend is particularly evident in German marques, with Porsche and Mercedes-Benz leading the charge. A standout example is the sale of a 1989 Rüf CTR Yellowbird, which fetched an impressive $6.055 million (£4.68 million) at Gooding's sale ([Zinio](https://www.zinio.com/gb/publications/classic-cars/5051)).

## Notable Events and Commemorations

The classic car community is also actively involved in charitable initiatives. The Classic Car Run, supporting the Aran Islands Lifeboat Station, celebrated its 10th year, demonstrating the community's commitment to philanthropy. This event not only raises funds but also brings together classic car enthusiasts for a noble cause ([RNLI](https://rnli.org/news-and-media/2025/may/06/classic-car-run-supports-aran-islands-lifeboat-station-for-the-10th-year)).

Furthermore, the Goodwood Members' Meeting continues to be a highlight in the classic car calendar. This year, the event featured a parade celebrating Alpine's 70th anniversary, showcasing 76 cars that span the Dieppe firm's entire history. The event also included high-speed demonstration runs and a timed shootout, underscoring the diversity and excitement of classic car racing ([Zinio](https://www.zinio.com/gb/publications/classic-sports-car/2188)).

## Commemorating Automotive History

The classic car community is also celebrating significant milestones in automotive history. This year marks the 100th anniversary of the Rolls-Royce Phantom, a testament to the enduring legacy of this iconic vehicle. The Phantom has long been regarded as a symbol of luxury and elegance, and its centenary is a cause for celebration among classic car enthusiasts ([Classic & Sports Car](https://www.classicandsportscar.com/news)).

## Conclusion

The classic car scene in the UK is vibrant and diverse, with events and trends that continue to captivate enthusiasts and collectors. From regional gatherings and charitable runs to high-profile auctions and historic commemorations, the classic car community is thriving. As we look to the future, it is clear that the passion for classic cars remains strong, driven by a deep appreciation for automotive history and a commitment to preserving the legacy of these timeless vehicles.
