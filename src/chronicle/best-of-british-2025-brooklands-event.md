## Introduction

The "Best of British" event at Brooklands Museum is a quintessential celebration of British automotive history and culture. Scheduled for May 26, 2025, this event promises a day filled with nostalgia, innovation, and family-friendly activities. Located in Weybridge, Surrey, the Brooklands Museum is a historic venue that embodies the spirit of British motorsport and engineering excellence. This report delves into the details of the event, the historical significance of the venue, and what attendees can expect from this celebration of British heritage.

## Event Overview

The "Best of British" event is set to take place on Bank Holiday Monday, May 26, 2025, from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The event will showcase a vast array of British-built vehicles, ranging from pre-war classics such as Alvis, Norton, and Sunbeam, to contemporary models from Jaguar and MG. This eclectic mix of automobiles highlights the evolution of British automotive engineering and design.

A unique aspect of this event is the participation of Paddington Bear, a beloved British character, who will be available for meet-and-greet sessions throughout the day. This addition underscores the event's family-friendly atmosphere, ensuring there's something for attendees of all ages.

## Historical Context

Brooklands Museum is a site of immense historical significance. For three decades, this racing track was at the heart of British motorsport. Located just 20 miles southwest of London, it played a crucial role in the development of automotive technology and racing culture in the UK. The museum now serves as a custodian of this rich heritage, preserving and showcasing the achievements of British engineering and motorsport.

## Attractions and Activities

The "Best of British" event offers a variety of attractions and activities designed to engage and entertain visitors. One of the highlights is the Test Hill ascents, where visitors can witness the power and performance of classic and modern vehicles as they tackle the challenging incline. This provides a dynamic demonstration of automotive capability and engineering prowess.

In addition to the vehicular displays and activities, the event will feature traditional British fare available in and around the Paddock, as well as at the on-site café. This culinary offering complements the cultural theme of the event, providing a taste of British cuisine to accompany the visual feast of classic automobiles.

## Logistics and Accessibility

The event will be held at Brooklands Museum, located at Brooklands Road, Weybridge, Surrey, KT13 0QN. For those using satellite navigation, the recommended postcode is KT13 0QN, with the What3words address being unions.added.finishing. Visitors arriving in pre-booked club vehicles can enter from 9:00 AM via the Campbell Gate entrance. General visitor parking will be available from 10:00 AM.

Tickets for the event are priced according to the museum's standard admission rates. Children under the age of four and museum members can attend for free, making it an affordable outing for families and car enthusiasts alike.

The "Best of British" <a href="https://classiccarclubs.uk/club-events/">event at Brooklands Museum</a> is more than just a car show; it is a celebration of British culture, history, and innovation. This event not only honours the past but also inspires future generations to appreciate and contribute to the ongoing narrative of British engineering excellence. With its rich history, engaging attractions, and family-friendly atmosphere, the "Best of British" event is set to be a highlight of the <a href="https://classiccarclubs.uk/club-events/">2025 calendar for automotive enthusiasts</a> and families alike.
