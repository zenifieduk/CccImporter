**Introduction**

The classic car market in the UK has always been a fascinating sector, reflecting a blend of nostalgia, investment potential, and automotive passion. As of Q1 2025, the landscape of this market is evolving, influenced by various economic, social, and technological factors. This report delves into the market trends, key factors influencing the market, popular classic car models, and price fluctuations, providing a comprehensive overview of the UK classic car market during this period.

**Market Trends**

![Maserati MC12 - a rare homologation version of the GT1 racing car that has seen a 53% increase in value year-on-year](/assets/images/market/maserati-mc12.avif)

<h3>Rising Values of Iconic Models</h3>

One of the most prominent trends in the UK classic car market is the rising value of iconic models such as the Porsche 911, particularly the performance iterations of the 996 and 997 generations. These models have seen their values increase significantly, demonstrating the enduring appeal and investment potential of well-regarded sports cars. The Maserati MC12, a rare homologation version of the GT1 racing car, stands out with a remarkable 53% increase in value year-on-year, adding £900,000 to its average value over the past 12 months.

<h3>Shifts in Buyer Demographics</h3>

The classic car market is witnessing a shift in buyer demographics, with a decline in younger buyers entering the market. This trend suggests that the surge in values for newer modern classics may not be as pronounced as in recent years. However, there remains a robust interest in sub-£50,000 'bargains,' which are attractive to first-time collectors and those seeking to enter the classic car market without substantial financial commitment.

<h3>Online Marketplaces and Auction Trends</h3>

The role of online marketplaces in the classic car market has become increasingly significant. In the UK, the auction site Collecting Cars has been pivotal, capturing some of the most expensive listings. Meanwhile, Car & Classic has sold more cars overall, indicating a healthy competition between platforms. In contrast, American sites such as Bring A Trailer have dominated the online auction space, with 8 of the top 10 sales occurring on these platforms.

**Key Factors Influencing the Market**

<h3>Economic Conditions and Consumer Behavior</h3>

The classic car market is influenced by broader economic conditions, including consumer confidence and disposable income levels. Economic fluctuations can lead to volatility in classic car values, as evidenced by the mixed performance of different segments. While Vintage European Exotics and Classic Trucks & SUVs have seen slight increases, segments such as Muscle Cars, Pony Cars, and American Classics have experienced declines.

### Sustainability and Technological Advancements 

The UK used car market is increasingly driven by sustainability and technological advancements, factors that also impact the classic car sector. The rise of electric vehicles (EVs) and hybrid models, coupled with the expansion of ULEZ zones, has altered buying patterns. Although this primarily affects modern vehicles, it highlights a growing consumer preference for environmentally friendly options, which could influence future classic car trends.

**Popular Classic Car Models**

In Q1 2025, certain classic car models have remained popular among collectors and enthusiasts. The Porsche 911, particularly the 996 and 997 generations, continues to be a sought-after model due to its performance capabilities and iconic status. The Maserati MC12, with its limited production and racing pedigree, has also captured significant attention and investment interest.

**Price Fluctuations**

Price fluctuations in the classic car market are influenced by several factors, including rarity, condition, and provenance. The Maserati MC12's substantial increase in value highlights the impact of rarity and historical significance on pricing. Conversely, segments such as American Classics have seen declines, partly due to changing collector demographics and interests.

**Conclusion**

The UK classic car market in Q1 2025 is characterised by rising values of iconic models, shifts in buyer demographics, and the growing influence of online marketplaces. Economic conditions, sustainability trends, and technological advancements are key factors shaping the market. While certain models like the Porsche 911 and Maserati MC12 continue to dominate, the market remains dynamic, with opportunities for both seasoned collectors and newcomers. As the year progresses, it will be crucial to monitor these trends and their implications for the classic car sector.
