## Market Overview

As the classic car market continues to thrive, June 2025 presents a vibrant scene for enthusiasts and collectors in the UK. This month showcases several notable auctions featuring a diverse array of classic vehicles, memorabilia, and automobilia. The following report provides an in-depth look at the major events, trends, and top-selling cars in the UK classic car auction market for June 2025.

## Major June 2025 Auctions

### Anglia Car Auctions: King's Lynn, Norfolk

<a href="/auction-houses/anglia-car-auctions/">Anglia Car Auctions</a>, a premier auction house located in King's Lynn, Norfolk, is hosting a two-day classic car auction on 14th-15th June 2025. Known for its extensive range of vehicles, Anglia Car Auctions will feature a wide selection of classic cars from various eras and price ranges. The event will also include automobilia on Saturday and motorcycles on Sunday, showcasing the auction house's commitment to catering to diverse collector interests.

### Barons Manor Park Classics: Southampton

On 14th June 2025, Barons <a href="/auction-houses/manor-park-classics/">Manor Park Classics</a> will conduct an auction in Southampton. Among the notable lots is a 1952 Austin A40 Sports Convertible, with a guide price ranging from £12,000 to £15,000. This auction is part of a broader trend of increasing interest in post-war British classics, which have seen a resurgence in popularity amongst collectors.

### Sworders Fine Art: Stansted Auction Rooms

Sworders Fine Art will host a classic car auction on 19th June 2025, at their Stansted Auction Rooms in Stansted Mountfitchet. The viewing times are scheduled from 13th-18th June, allowing potential buyers ample opportunity to inspect the vehicles. This auction is expected to draw significant interest, given the reputation of Sworders for curating high-quality classic car auctions.

### H&H Classic Car Auctions: Millbrook Proving Grounds

On 25th June 2025, <a href="/auction-houses/h-and-h-classics/">H&H Classic Car Auctions</a> will hold an event at the Millbrook Proving Grounds near Bedford. This auction marks a significant occasion as it is hosted at a new venue, which is expected to attract a wide audience of collectors and enthusiasts. The auction is anticipated to feature a range of classic cars, emphasising the continued interest and investment in vintage automobiles.

### Mathewsons Classic Cars: Pickering

Mathewsons Classic Cars is preparing for a substantial auction event from 25th-27th June 2025, at their site in Pickering. This auction will feature approximately 900 lots, including 300-400 cars and motorbikes. The selection includes iconic brands such as Land Rover, Ford, Mercedes, Jaguar, Aston Martin, and Mini. The 1989 Mercedes 260 E Auto is amongst the highlighted vehicles, reflecting the diversity and richness of the auction lineup.

### Barn-Find Auction: South Gloucestershire

A unique barn-find auction is scheduled for 28th June 2025, in South Gloucestershire. This event is poised to be one of Britain's largest barn-find auctions, featuring over 200 vehicles. Such auctions are particularly appealing to collectors seeking restoration projects or rare finds, and this event is expected to draw considerable interest from across the UK.

## Notable Vehicles and Highlights


### Post-War British Classics
The 1952 Austin A40 Sports Convertible at Barons represents the resurgent interest in post-war British sports cars. With its £12,000-£15,000 guide price, it exemplifies the accessible end of the classic market.

### German Engineering Excellence
Mathewsons' 1989 Mercedes 260 E Auto showcases the growing collector interest in 1980s German saloons, reflecting the market's expansion beyond traditional British marques.

### Barn-Find Treasures
The South Gloucestershire barn-find auction promises over 200 vehicles, potentially including rare discoveries and restoration projects that could yield significant returns for knowledgeable buyers.

## Market Trends and Insights

The classic car auction market in the UK continues to demonstrate robust health, with strong demand for both iconic and rare models. Auctions in June 2025 reflect a diverse range of vehicles, from post-war British classics to rare barn finds and restored American muscle cars. Collectors are increasingly interested in vehicles with historical significance or unique provenance, driving prices upward for well-preserved and documented examples.

Moreover, the inclusion of automobilia and memorabilia in auctions indicates a growing interest in the broader culture and history of motoring. This trend is likely to continue, as collectors seek to complement their vehicle acquisitions with related artefacts and collectibles.

### Key Market Observations
- **Diversification**: Buyers are looking beyond traditional British marques to include German and American classics
- **Restoration Projects**: Barn-find auctions are gaining popularity as collectors seek project cars
- **Memorabilia Growth**: Automobilia sales reflect broader cultural interest in motoring heritage
- **Venue Innovation**: New locations like Millbrook Proving Grounds attract fresh audiences
